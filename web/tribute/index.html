Timeline

The short version

  1. 1998

    Born in Chicago

    Jarad Anthony Higgins was born on December 2 and grew up around Chicago. He played piano, drums and guitar as a kid.

  2. 2015

    JuicetheKidd on SoundCloud

    He started posting tracks to SoundCloud as JuicetheKidd, then shortened the name to Juice WRLD.

  3. 2017

    “Lucid Dreams” and “All Girls Are the Same”

    Both songs took off. He signed with Grade A Productions and Interscope not long after.

  4. 2018

    Goodbye & Good Riddance

    The debut album came out in May. That same year he recorded an hour-long freestyle for Tim Westwood, all of it off the top.

  5. 2019

    Death Race for Love, then the end

    His second album debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 in March. He died on December 8 at Chicago’s Midway Airport, of an accidental overdose. He was 21.

  6. 2020 to 2024

    Legends Never Die

    Three more albums came out after he died, built from recordings he had already made. He left behind a lot of unreleased material.

The number

Flip the 666

He had it tattooed, said it on records, and put it on the merch. It was his answer to 666: take the worst thing that happened to you and flip it into something you can use.

  • He used songs to say things he didn’t say anywhere else.
  • He freestyled his verses live instead of writing them.
  • Fans still write 999 on things, and still mean what he meant by it.

Legacy

Live free, 999

After he died, his mother Carmela Wallace started the Live Free 999 Fund. It supports young people dealing with anxiety, depression and substance use, which is most of what he wrote about.

If you’re struggling with your mental health or with using, you don’t have to deal with it on your own. Talk to someone you trust, or call a crisis line. In the US and Canada you can call or text 988.
